# STEP 16 - Dependency requests

**R1 Write operations for clients, invoices, products and estimates (also needed for invoice drafts and credit notes of store orders, 16.5).** The `clients` and `invoices` contracts only read (`getById`, `getBilling`, `getForPayment`, `applyPayment`, `markDisputed`, `snapshot`). The public API, the e-commerce order sync and the migration wizard must create and update records through the owning services. Please add to `types/contracts/`: `clients.create|update|findByEmail`, `invoices.createDraft|getById|createPaymentLink`, `products.create|update|findBySku` and `estimates.getById` (all with a company id from the tenant context, optimistic version, an idempotency key, and the same audit and event behaviour as the STEP 08 and 09 services). Until then STEP 16 offers only read endpoints and stops the write parts with `BLOCKERS.md`.

**R2 OCR and bank feed hand-over.** STEP 15 asked that STEP 16 call `applyOcrResult` (`services/expense/receipts.ts`) and `importFeedBatch` (`services/reconciliation/transactions.ts`) directly. They are the only direct cross-STEP calls of STEP 16 and sit behind `services/ocr/ports.ts` and `services/bankfeed/ports.ts`. If the Integrator prefers events (`receipt.ocr_completed`, `bank_feed.batch_imported`), add the names to `types/events.ts`; the ports are the only files that change.

**R3 Packages.** None is required to start (REST through `fetch`). If the owner wants the official Plaid or Google Vision clients, a QR image library or an SDK build, they are requested here before use.

**R4 Cron file names.** This STEP names cron files `cron/jobs/s16-*.ts` like the earlier STEPs; SCOPE.md says `16-*`. Confirm that `s16-*` is right for `scripts/gen.mjs`.

**R5 Content events for social rules.** Auto-post rules (16.4.b) should start by themselves when a blog post is published or a product is added. The event list (`types/events.ts`) has no `blog.published`, `product.created` or similar name, and STEP 16 may not invent one. Until the owning STEPs (06 CMS, 08 products) publish such events, a rule is started by an authenticated call, `POST /api/social/rules/fire` with a trigger key and a source id. If the Integrator adds the events, one worker file in `workers/integrations/` can call the same service (`fireTrigger`) with the payload mapped to `title`, `url`, `summary` and `imageUrl`; nothing else changes.

**R6 E-mail template catalogue.** Multi-channel messaging (16.9) sends e-mail with the template `integrations.broadcast` (subject `{subject}`, variables `subject` and `body`), defined in `emails/integrations/index.ts` as `integrationEmailTemplates`. Please add one line to `emails/catalog.ts`: `import { integrationEmailTemplates } from './integrations';` and `...integrationEmailTemplates,` in the list. Until then the e-mail service answers that the template does not exist and the e-mail channel of messaging records a failed attempt.

**R7 Import targets of STEP 08 for the migration wizard.** The migration wizard (16.10) makes clients, products and suppliers through the owning services by calling STEP 08 directly in one file, `services/migration/ports.ts`: `getImportTarget` (the check of a row, the search for a duplicate and the creation of a record, as the STEP 08 import framework uses them), `readXlsx`, `parseCsv`, `splitTable`, `applyMapping`, `missingRequired`, and the delete operations `deleteClient`, `deleteProduct` and `deleteSupplier` (used by a rollback). There is no contract for this (request R1 asked for client, product and invoice writes and is still open). Please either confirm these calls, or offer a contract (for example `imports.target(id)` and `<list>.remove`); only `ports.ts` changes. Invoices, payments, opening balances of accounts and historical payments need the write operations of R1 and are not imported.

**R8 DELETE through the guarded transport.** `lib/security/ssrf.ts` (`safeHttpsRequest`) knows GET, POST and HEAD. Salt Edge removes a connection with `DELETE /connections/{id}` (that also revokes the consent and stops the data), so the bank feed (16.7) cannot call it and only stops using the link; the owner is told to remove it in the Salt Edge dashboard. Please add `'DELETE'` to the methods of `safeHttpsRequest` (`SafeRequestOptions.method`) and to `Transport` in `lib/integrations/commerce-http.ts` can then be widened; the call is `removeConnection` in `lib/integrations/saltedge.ts` and the line in `disconnect` of `services/bankfeed/connections.ts`.
