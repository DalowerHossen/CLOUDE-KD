# STEP 13 - Handoff: client portal, contracts and disputes

Branch `claude/clever-goldberg-8ncdae` (continues the STEP 02 to 12 line; see D1). Sub-steps 13.1 to 13.10 are done (PROGRESS.md). Decisions D1 to D37 are in DECISIONS.md, requests R1 to R8 in DEPENDENCY_REQUESTS.md, verification in VERIFY.md.

## What this STEP provides
**Client pages (no login, tokenized links, `noindex`, no analytics)**: `/i/<token>` invoice, `/pay/<token>` payment (consent first, then the STEP 10 checkout), `/e/<token>` estimate (accept or decline with a typed name), `/r/<token>` receipt (and "ask for a refund"), `/hub/<token>` all invoices of a client, `/c/<token>` contract signing (typed, drawn or uploaded signature, decline), `/expired` re-request. Every page checks the link through the tokens contract, limits requests per address and per link, shows one answer for any bad link, and records what the client did. Links of contracts always need a one-time code. Company look (colours, font, logo, footer) is applied inside every page; on a company's own verified domain only that company's links work.

**Owner pages**: `/client-mail` (messages sent to clients with delivery status, bulk estimate reminders), `/client-mail/requests` (approve or deny "my link expired"), `/client-mail/branding`, `/contracts` (list, new, detail with signers, history, signed copy and seal check), `/disputes` (list and numbers), `/disputes/<id>` (status workflow, reason and date, notes, evidence pack), `/disputes/refund-requests`, and for the platform team `/admin/disputes` (read only).

**Services**: `services/portal` (access, events, OTP session, consent, pay link, invoice, estimate, receipt, hub, branding hooks, client mail and delivery proof, recipients, link requests, notices, bulk mail), `services/contract`, `services/esign`, `services/dispute`, `services/evidence`, `services/branding`. PDFs: `lib/pdf/receipt-pdf.tsx`, `lib/pdf/evidence-pack-pdf.tsx`, `services/esign/contract-pdf.tsx`. The PDF renderer `receipt` is registered on the PDF contract (`services/portal/register.ts`); the brand resolver for e-mail (`services/branding/register.ts`) and the OTP recipient for contract links (`services/contract/register.ts`) are registered at boot.

## Routes (API)
Public (token in the body, never in the address): `/api/portal/{otp/send,otp/verify,rerequest,consent,pay-link,pdf,hub/open,estimate/decide,contract/sign,contract/decline,refund-request}`, and `GET /api/portal/logo/<company>` (the company logo, images only).
Owner: `/api/portal/{delivery,branding,branding/{reset,import,history},link-requests,link-requests/<id>,bulk/estimates}`, `/api/contracts` (+ `/<id>`, `/send`, `/request-send`, `/remind`, `/void`, `/versions`, `/events`, `/signed`, `/templates`), `/api/disputes` (+ `/<id>`, `/move`, `/note`, `/details`, `/refund-requests`, `/evidence`, `/evidence/<id>/<pdf|zip>`).

Constants: `config/s13-portal.ts` (consent version, payment page timeout, pay-link and hub-link lifetimes, dispute answer window and alert hours).

## Evidence pack (nine sections)
1 invoice as sent (with the checksum of the stored sent-PDF), 2 receipt, 3 consent (exact words, version, checksum, time, address, device) with the refund policy, 4 delivery proof, 5 communication log, 6 client identity (address, country, pages viewed, 3D Secure result), 7 timeline, 8 audit chain proof (chain check and entry hashes), 9 platform details. Made by one click as PDF and ZIP (also: sent invoice PDF, receipt PDF, `data.json`, processor format when the adapter has one, `manifest.json` with SHA-256 of each file, README). Stored through the storage contract in the category `evidence` (7 years in `config/storage.ts`); the pack promises 18 months (`PACK_RETENTION_MONTHS`). Checksums of the PDF and the ZIP are written to the audit chain (`dispute.evidence_pack_created`).
