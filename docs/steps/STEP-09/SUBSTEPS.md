Migrations `S09_001` onward with `_down.sql`; assertions `sql/assertions/s09_*.sql`; logic checks `scripts/verify/step09-*`. Per sub-step: prettier on touched files, `npm run gen`, typecheck, lint (zero warnings), `scripts/check-all.sh`, `node scripts/verify/step04-static.mjs`, `PGHOST=/tmp/pgsock PGUSER=postgres bash scripts/db-apply.sh --clean` when a migration exists, security checklist Z1 to Z14, one commit `feat(step09): 09.k <summary>`.

Owned paths (OWNERSHIP_MAP row 09): `app/(dashboard)/{dashboard,invoices,estimates,projects,reports}/`, `services/{invoice,estimate,project,tax,fx,report}/`, `lib/pdf/` (engine, invoice, estimate, report), `pdf-templates/`, `app/api/{invoices,estimates,projects,reports,pdf}/`, `components/{invoice,pdf,charts}/`, `emails/invoice/`, `migrations/S09_*`. Extra paths (pure logic, config fragments, verify scripts, workers) follow the `NN` naming and are added to `config/ownership.json`: `lib/{invoice,billing,tax,fx,reports}/**`, `workers/{invoice,billing}/**`, `components/{project,report}/**` is not needed (charts live in `components/charts`).

Design rules:

- Money is `numeric(18,4)` and handled only with `Money` (decimal.js). Quantities `numeric(18,4)`; percentages `numeric(7,4)`. Rates are frozen on the invoice.
- Tenant comes from the session; a foreign id answers 404. Every table has the standard columns, RLS default deny, the audit trigger, `version`; editable records are saved with `expectedVersion`. Every service query names the company (checked by a static script like `step08-tenant.mjs`).
- Numbers are gapless per company, series and year; the next number is taken inside the transaction that creates the document under an advisory lock (`kd_next_doc_number`).
- A sent invoice is immutable: a database trigger refuses edits of money and line columns once the status leaves draft. Snapshots (immutable PDF plus JSON) are stored through the `storage` contract and recorded in `invoice_snapshots`, which refuses update and delete.
- Other STEPs are reached only through contracts: `clients` (STEP 08), `storage`, `email`, `notification`, `tokens`, `approval`, `events`, `jobs`, `locks`. Payments (STEP 10) call `invoices.applyPayment`; STEP 09 never reads payment tables. Where data of STEP 10 or 15 is needed (payment method breakdown, expenses), STEP 09 reads a small registry and shows "no data" until the provider registers (DEPENDENCY_REQUESTS).
- Public client pages (view invoice, approve estimate with signature) belong to STEP 13. STEP 09 issues tokens through the `tokens` contract and provides the service functions the portal calls.
- Report screens are rate limited and audited; exports are watermarked like STEP 08 exports.
