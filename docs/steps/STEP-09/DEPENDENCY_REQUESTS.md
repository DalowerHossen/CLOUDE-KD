No new packages. STEP 09 uses packages already in `package.json`: `@react-pdf/renderer` (PDF), `sharp` (logo size), `recharts` (charts), `@react-email/components` (e-mail), `decimal.js` through `lib/utils/currency.ts`, `zod`. 6. Environment: one optional variable `PDF_FONT_DIR` (`lib/env/s09.ts`, `config/env/s09.env.fragment`). A folder with `regular.ttf` and `bold.ttf` of a Unicode font makes PDFs show non-Latin names. Without it PDFs use Helvetica and show "?" for characters it cannot draw. Fonts are not committed (size and licence); please choose the font and mount the folder.

## For other STEPs

- STEP 10 to 12 (payments): call `getService('invoices').getForPayment(invoiceId)` for the amount due and `applyPayment({ invoiceId, paymentId, amount })` when a payment settles (applying twice with the same `paymentId` changes nothing). Cash reports of STEP 09 count every payment link that is not a credit, deposit or write-off. A report "payments by method" can be added with `registerReport` in `services/report/reports.ts`.
- STEP 13 (client pages): invoice link `/i/<token>`, estimate link `/e/<token>` (links are built in `lib/billing/links.ts`; tokens come from the `tokens` contract with `docType` `invoice` and `estimate`). Functions to call: `recordInvoiceViewed(invoiceId)`, `getEstimateByToken(token)` and `decideEstimate(token, { decision, signerName, note, agree }, { ip, agent })` (the caller passes the network address and browser from the request). The invoice PDF of a sent invoice is the stored snapshot (`invoice_snapshots`, read with `readSnapshot` in `services/invoice/snapshots.ts`); `getService('pdf').render('invoice', { invoiceId })` draws a fresh one.
- STEP 14 (subscriptions) and STEP 15 (accounting): `registerReport({ key, title, description, category, usesPeriod, groupable, run })` adds reports (expenses, profit and loss, cash flow, payroll) to the report catalogue; `registerBillableExpenseProvider` (`services/project/expense-hook.ts`) lets approved reimbursable expenses be billed on a project invoice. Journal entries for invoices, credit notes, write-offs and deposits can be built from `invoices`, `credit_notes`, `credit_applications` (source types `credit_note`, `deposit`, `write_off`) and `invoice_payment_links`.
- STEP 08 (stock): the worker `workers/inventory/invoice-stock.event.ts` already takes stock on `invoice.sent` and gives it back on `invoice.voided`; both carry `lines` (see item 9 for credit notes).
- STEP 07: STEP 09 tables are registered for tenant exports in `services/invoice/tenant-export.ts` (checked against the database by `scripts/verify/step09-exports.sh`; the seller's bank details, signature checksums and network addresses are not exported).
