# STEP 09 decisions

- D1 (plan) The SCOPE lists 11 sub-steps; they are split into 17 so that each stays under 15 files and one migration group. Order follows dependencies: calculation, tax and FX, invoice core, numbering, screens, send and contract, PDF, then the document families, billing lifecycle, projects, dashboard, reports, batch, closure.
- D2 (plan) Public client pages (viewing an invoice by token, approving an estimate with a signature) are STEP 13 (`app/(client-portal)`, services `portal` and `esign`). STEP 09 issues tokens through the `tokens` contract and provides the service functions the portal calls; the missing contract operations (mark viewed, record an estimate decision) go to DEPENDENCY_REQUESTS.
- D3 (plan) Data that belongs to STEP 10 (payments by method and gateway) and STEP 15 (expenses, cash flow, AP aging, accounting) reaches the reports only through a provider registry in `lib/reports/providers.ts`; until a provider registers, those report sections show a clear "no data from that module yet" state. STEP 09 never reads another STEP's tables.
- D4 (09.1) Calculation engine (`lib/invoice/calc.ts`, pure, decimal arithmetic only). Per line: price times quantity, then the line discount (percent or a fixed amount that can never turn the line around), then the share of the document discount (a fixed document discount is shared over the lines with a positive amount in proportion to their amounts; the last line takes the remainder so the shares add up exactly), then tax. Discount order is a setting: "before tax" (discounts lower the taxable amount) or "after tax" (tax is worked out on the amount before discounts; the discount only lowers what is payable). Prices may include tax ("inclusive"); the tax is then taken out with an exact factor (`taxMultiplier`, twelve decimal places, integer arithmetic) and the last tax absorbs the rounding so net plus tax is exactly the entered price. Several taxes per line, each marked compound or not (a compound tax is worked out on the amount plus the taxes before it). A tax-exempt line carries no tax.
- D5 (09.1) Rounding is a document setting: round every line and tax ("per line", default) or only the document totals ("on total"; the engine then reports `roundingDifference`, the gap between the rounded lines and the rounded total, for the reconciliation line); rule "half up" or banker's "half even"; precision 2, 3 or 4 places. Six places are not offered: money is stored as numeric(18,4). Optional cash rounding of the final total to 0.05, 0.10, 0.50 or 1 is shown as its own adjustment line. Withholding tax is a percentage of the amount without tax (including shipping); it lowers `amountDue` but not the total.
- D6 (09.1) Amount in words is English ("One Hundred Twenty-Three USD and Forty-Five Cents Only"), up to 15 digits, "Minus" for negatives. The verification script `scripts/verify/step09-calc.ts` holds the hand-calculated cases (0.1 + 0.2, inclusive tax, discount order, compound tax, rounding policies, shipping, withholding, cash rounding, negative and zero invoices) and a check on 2000 generated invoices that the parts always add up to the total.
- D7 (09.2) Tax data (migration S09_001): `tax_rates` (code unique per company, percentage `numeric(7,4)`, kinds sales, VAT, GST, withholding and other, optional compound flag, country and region, active flag), `tax_settings` (one row per company: price mode, discount order, rounding policy, rule and places, cash rounding, financial year start, seller tax number and country, EU reverse-charge switch, marketplace-facilitator declaration, US sales-tax states, default tax codes on new lines, default withholding) and `client_tax_exemptions` (exempt flag with reason, certificate file through the storage contract, certificate expiry, client tax number). The calculation defaults of D4 and D5 are read from `tax_settings`. A change to a rate never changes an existing invoice: invoices copy the rates they use. Country templates (India GST, Malaysia SST, Vietnam VAT, Singapore, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Japan, South Korea, Bangladesh, Pakistan, China, United Kingdom, Australia, United Arab Emirates) are one-click suggestions to be checked with a tax adviser; India e-invoicing (IRN), fapiao and TDS/TCS filing are not built (SCOPE: display only).
- D8 (09.2) EU reverse charge is a pure rule (`reverseChargeApplies`): switched on in the settings, seller and client in different EU countries, client has a tax number. The VAT-number check (VIES) is a hook (`registerTaxIdChecker`); without a registered service the number is stored as entered and shown as "not checked". The tax pages live under `/invoices/tax` (the settings area of STEP 07 is not ours).
- D9 (09.2) Exchange rates (`fx_rates`, units of currency per US dollar, `numeric(20,8)`): platform rates have no company, a company can type in its own rates; the newest rate on or before the asked day wins, and on the same day the company's rate wins (`kd_fx_rate`). Stored money stays USD; conversion is for display only (`convertUsd`, two places, none for JPY, VND and IDR). The daily job `fx-rates` asks the registered rate providers (`lib/fx/provider.ts`). No provider is registered: the rate source's own documentation could not be reached from this environment (the host is blocked), and a provider must be written from that documentation, so until then companies type their own rates (DEPENDENCY_REQUESTS). The `fx` contract is registered in `services/fx/register.ts`. FX gain and loss belongs to accounting (STEP 15).
- D10 (09.3) Invoice tables (migration S09_002): `invoices` (header with frozen calculation options, frozen totals, tax breakdown, bill-to and ship-to blocks, seller block frozen at send, display currency with the frozen rate, duplicate hash, status fields), `invoice_items` (lines with their worked-out amounts and the tax rates copied onto the line), `invoice_revisions` and `invoice_events` (append only), `invoice_payment_links` (one row per payment applied, unique per payment and invoice, so applying twice is harmless). One table holds invoices, proforma invoices, deposit invoices and debit notes (`kind`); credit notes and estimates have their own tables later. The invoice number is empty on a draft and is given when the invoice is sent (09.4), so numbers have no gaps.
- D11 (09.3) A sent invoice is a legal record. The database trigger `kd_invoice_guard` lets a draft change freely; once the invoice is waiting for approval, sent or later, only status fields, payment fields, internal notes, tags, category and the approval and sending fields can change - client, dates, amounts, rates, notes seen by the client are refused (error 42501), the number and the seller block cannot change once set, and a sent invoice cannot be deleted (only a void one can go to the trash). Lines are locked by `kd_invoice_item_guard` as soon as the invoice leaves draft. Corrections to a sent invoice are made with a credit note or by voiding and writing a new invoice.
- D12 (09.3) The server never trusts totals: the input has no total fields; `prepareInvoice` turns tax codes into the company's current rates (copied onto the lines), applies a client's tax exemption (unless the certificate has expired) and the EU reverse charge (all tax removed, flag kept), freezes the exchange rate for the display currency on the issue date (error when no rate exists) and calculates with the engine of D4. The calculation options (price mode, discount order, rounding) are copied from the tax settings by the screen and stored on the invoice, so a later change of settings never changes an old invoice. Bill-to details are typed or copied by the screen from the client record (the `clients` contract only offers name and billing postal code and country); the contract is used to check that the client is the company's own.
- D13 (09.3) Revisions: every manual save (and create, and a restore) writes a revision holding the full input; the automatic save every 30 seconds updates the draft without a revision. A revision can be restored into the draft (as a new save). Status override is owner-only, needs a reason, follows the status machine (`lib/invoice/status.ts`; a sent invoice never returns to draft, a paid invoice can only become disputed, a void invoice is final) and is audited. Money columns use the helper `toInt` of `lib/reports/numbers.ts` for whole numbers because the money guard forbids `Number()` in files with money words in their path.
- D14 (09.4) Numbering (migration S09_003): `invoice_sequences` holds one counter per company, series (INV, PI, DEP, DN) and year (0 when the series never resets). `kd_invoice_assign_number` locks the invoice, takes the next value under an advisory lock per company and series (`kd_next_doc_number`) and writes the number on the invoice in the same transaction, so a failed save gives the number back and parallel requests never share one (proved by `scripts/verify/step09-numbering.sh`: 100 parallel numbers are 1 to 100 with no gap, a rolled-back number is reused). A draft has no number; it is given at send or approval (09.6). Format and start come from the company's invoice settings of STEP 07 (prefix, yearly or continuous numbering, digits, first number); the first number of the company starts the series, a new year of a yearly series starts at 1, and the year is the year of sending, not of the issue date. Proforma, deposit and debit note series use the fixed prefixes PI-, DEP- and DN-. Voided documents keep their number; numbers are never reused. The owner can type a number on a draft (unique per company and kind, uses no counter value, cannot be typed once the invoice is sent).
- D15 (09.5) Invoice list: search runs in the database function `kd_invoice_search` (migration S09_004): number, purchase order number, client name with typo tolerance (word similarity), exact amount; filters for state (one or several), type, client, issue date range and amount range; sort by issue date, due date, total, state, client, number or creation; cursor pages of 25, 50 or 100; the trash view lists deleted drafts and void invoices. Rows can be shown as a table or as cards. A "Late" mark shows on open invoices past their due date. Bulk selection and bulk actions come with 09.16.
- D16 (09.5) Invoice form: everything the client side shows is only a preview. The form works the totals out with the same engine (`previewTotals`), but the server works them out again on every save. The form copies the client's bill-to and ship-to blocks and usual payment terms when a client is chosen (through the client API of STEP 08, read-only), supports walk-in customers, dynamic lines (items from the catalogue picker with the client's price, free lines, move up and down, drag to reorder, per-line discount, tax chips and a "no tax" switch), windowed rendering above 60 lines so an invoice with thousands of lines stays fast, a live totals panel with the amount in words, the tax breakdown and the display-currency amount at the frozen rate, and an automatic save every 30 seconds for an existing draft (no revision is written by an automatic save). A new invoice is created with an Idempotency-Key so a double click makes one draft. Conflicts show the plain "someone else changed this" message. The invoice page shows the same form read-only for sent invoices, with the history (timeline and earlier versions with a comparison and a restore button for drafts) and the actions (copy, trash, restore, owner-only manual number and manual status).
