# STEP 15 - Decisions

D1 (2026-10-23). Where the code lives. `OWNERSHIP_MAP.md` gives STEP 15 no `lib/accounting/`, so the pure rules (input shapes, balance checks, CSV reading, tree, posting maths) live in `services/accounting/*-rules.ts` files that do not import `server-only`, and constants in `config/s15-accounting.ts` (the `sNN-` rule). Verification scripts are `scripts/verify/step15-*.ts`; strings will be `lib/i18n/strings/en/s15-accounting.ts` (15.4.b with the pages).

D2 (2026-10-23). Chart and journals (15.1). Migration `S15_001_chart_journals`. One chart per company; the platform has no shared chart. `accounts` has type, subtype (the pairs are checked in the database), a contra flag (accumulated depreciation, refunds), a generated `normal_side`, a parent (same company and type, at most 6 levels, no loop), `allow_posting` (false for group accounts) and system accounts (`is_system` with a `system_key`) that keep their code, type and key and can never be removed. An account with journal lines keeps its type and cannot be removed; it can be made inactive. Changes are written to the append-only `account_history`. `account_mappings` says which account a kind of transaction posts to (19 keys in `config/s15-accounting.ts`); the base chart fills them and the owner can re-map. A merge of two accounts moves the child accounts and the mappings to the target and makes the source inactive; posted lines are never touched, because history never changes (reports can group by the merge history in 15.4). The starting chart is the base chart plus an industry add-on (general, service, retail, medical, SaaS) and a country template that renames accounts to the words of US GAAP, IFRS or India (codes stay the same so reports stay comparable). The chart is imported from QuickBooks or Xero style CSV files and exported as CSV (a cell that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is prefixed so a spreadsheet cannot run it).

D3 (2026-10-23). Entries (15.1). An entry is `draft`, `pending_approval` or `posted`. Debit equals credit, two lines at least and a total above zero are checked by a database trigger when an entry leaves draft, so no code path can post an unbalanced entry. The lines of an entry that is not a draft cannot change (insert, update and the soft delete of the guard are refused); a posted entry changes only to be marked as reversed. The number `JE-YYYY-NNNNNN` (year of the entry date) is given when the entry is posted (`kd_journal_number`, a counter per company and year). `kd_journal_create` makes the entry and its lines in one step; with a source key it makes one entry per key (a replay returns the first one), which is what the automated posting of 15.2 uses. A manual or imported entry with the same date and lines as an earlier live entry is refused with `KD001` unless the owner confirms (then it is linked in `duplicate_of`). Reversal (`kd_journal_reverse`) makes a new posted entry with debit and credit swapped, linked both ways, once only; a reversing entry cannot be reversed. A draft is replaced line by line (`kd_journal_replace`, with the version check) and can be removed; a posted entry cannot be removed. Approval: an owner posts at once; a staff member's post request becomes `pending_approval` with an approval request `journal.post` (level L1, 48 hours) and the owner decides; a rejection returns the entry to draft. Journal templates (reusable entries), CSV bulk import (creates drafts, one entry per reference) and attachments (storage contract, category `attachment`, PDF, PNG, JPEG or CSV up to 8 MB; a file of a posted entry cannot be removed) are in 15.1. Recurring entries (feature 2984) come with the periods job in 15.3 and the accountant review queue (3004) with automated posting in 15.2. The period check (feature 2991) is added by the trigger of 15.3.

D4 (2026-10-23). Automated posting (15.2). Migration `S15_002_posting`. The posting rules table is code (`services/accounting/posting-rules.ts`, `POSTING_RULES`), not a database table: it lists nine events and each one's lines by transaction key, and the company's `account_mappings` (15.1) turn the keys into accounts. A company that never opened accounting gets the starting chart the first time an event arrives; a company whose chart lacks a needed account gets it from the base chart (a free number if the usual one is taken). Two new keys: `wallet_balance` (1170, money settled into the company wallet and not yet paid out) and `chargeback_expense` (6410). One handler (`workers/s15/accounting-posting.event.ts`) reads the events; each event makes one entry, once per business source key (`invoice:<id>:sent`, `payment:<id>:captured`, `refund:<id>`, `payout:<id>:paid`, `dispute:<id>:lost`, `expense:<id>`, `payroll:<run>:approved|paid`), so a second delivery or a second event for the same thing changes nothing; the event id is kept in the posting log, which has one row per event and rule. The rules:
- Invoice: posted when it is sent (a draft is not revenue): debit receivable (total), credit revenue (total less tax), credit tax payable. A voided invoice reverses that entry (once; "never posted" and "already reversed" are logged as skipped). Withholding tax on an invoice is not split out (the receivable is the full total).
- Payment captured: the settlement of STEP 14 decides the books. Direct: debit bank, credit receivable. Through the wallet (including a suspended company, whose money the platform holds): debit wallet balance (net), debit payment processing fees (gross less net), credit receivable (gross). If the settlement is not recorded yet, the handler fails and the event is delivered again.
- Refund: debit refunds (contra revenue), credit bank (direct) or wallet balance. A fee returned by the platform is not booked separately.
- Payout paid: debit bank (net), debit processing fees (payout fee), credit wallet balance (amount). A failed or cancelled payout never reached the books; a payout reversed after payment has no event in the contract (request R1).
- Dispute lost: debit chargebacks, credit bank or wallet balance, amount from the dispute case.
- Expense and payroll: the payload is defined here and 15.5 and 15.8 must publish it (expense: `expenseId, date, net, tax, accountId?, paid, memo`; pay run: `runId, periodEnd, gross, employerCost, net` and `runId, net, paidOn`). Payroll liabilities hold net pay plus the tax and deductions withheld and the employer cost; paying the net pay clears part of it, the rest stays until it is remitted.
- Credit notes, debit notes, fixed assets, depreciation and FX gain or loss (features 2998 to 3002) need events the contract does not have or belong to 15.7; see R1.
A failed posting is logged with its reason and retried by the event runner; the owner sees failed and skipped rows, can retry one after fixing the chart, and reviews what the system posted (the accountant review queue, feature 3004: posted rows start unreviewed and are marked reviewed in bulk). Posting dates follow the event; a closed-period rule is added by 15.3.
