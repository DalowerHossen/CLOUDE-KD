# STEP 08 decisions

- D1 (08.1) Clients are one table `clients` with the billing and shipping address as flat columns, tags as a text array and custom field values as small JSON; the contact people are rows in `client_contacts` (the first one is the primary contact), replaced in one database step. The billing postal code and country are required when a client is created or changed (REQ-VERIFY compares them with the payer); the quick form used while writing an invoice asks only for name, optional e-mail, postal code and country. The database allows an empty value so that imported and anonymized records can exist, and the contract returns null in that case.
- D2 (08.1) Later STEPs reach the client record through three things only: the `clients` contract (`getById`, `getBilling`), the events `client.created` and `client.updated`, and the registries in `lib/crm/hooks.ts` (delete guard, merge handler, timeline, statement, export, anonymize). The invoice STEP must register a delete guard (a client with invoices cannot be deleted, only archived); until it does, delete is allowed. The contract checks the company of the signed-in caller when there is one, so a foreign client is "not found".
- D3 (08.1) Custom fields (`custom_field_defs`) are shared by clients, products and suppliers: the owner defines key, label, kind (text, number, date, choice, yes/no), choices and "required"; values are checked against the definitions on every save and unknown keys are refused. Money in the client record (opening balance, credit limit, credit balance) is decimal text in the API and `numeric(18,4)` in the database; the credit balance is changed by the payments STEP, not by the form.
- D4 (08.2) Search is one database function (`kd_client_search`): pg_trgm similarity plus plain "contains" on name, e-mail, phone, contact person and tax number, filters (state, tag, country, risk, "owes money"), four sort orders (name, date added, balance owed, last invoice) and keyset pages (the cursor is the last row's sort value and id), always filtered by company. Wildcard characters typed by the user are plain text. Balance and last invoice come from columns on the client record that the invoice and payment STEPs keep current with `kd_client_set_balances` (they are the only writers); until then they stay zero.
- D5 (08.2) Duplicates: creating a client first asks `kd_client_duplicates` (same e-mail, same tax number, or name similarity of 0.6 or more). If anything matches, the server answers 409 `duplicate` with the candidates and the form offers "Create anyway" (`confirmDuplicate: true`). A review page lists look-alike pairs. Merge (owner only, fresh step-up) first calls the merge handlers other STEPs register (they point their records to the kept client), then one database step fills the empty fields of the kept client, combines tags and contacts (ten at most), adds balances, moves the history, soft deletes the other client and stores a snapshot in `client_merges`.
- D6 (08.2) Timeline: notes, calls, meetings and e-mail entries written in this STEP, system entries (created, archived, reactivated, merged), plus whatever the registered timeline providers return. Statement: opening balance plus open documents and payments from registered providers, aging buckets (not due, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 to 90, over 90 days) added with decimal arithmetic. The statement PDF and e-mail need the `pdf` contract of STEP 09, so the statement is a printable page for now.
- D7 (08.3) Client data management: per-client export is one JSON file (record, contacts, activity, merges, privacy requests, plus sections that other STEPs add through export providers); it is audited and uses the strict rate policy. Marketing consent and the preferred way of contact are fields of the client; turning consent on or off is also written to the consent log (subject `client:<id>`, purpose `email_marketing`). "Do not contact" blocks the statement e-mail.
- D8 (08.3) Privacy requests are the company's own (the company is the controller of its clients' data), so they do not use the platform data-subject request table. Anonymize replaces names, contacts, addresses, notes, free text history and merge snapshots, keeps ids, country and all amounts so the accounts still add up, and marks the record. Delete does the same and also removes the client from the lists, and is refused while a delete guard says something depends on the client. The owner acts directly after a fresh step-up; a staff request goes through the approval engine (`client.anonymize`, level L1) and the job `client-privacy` (every 5 minutes) applies the owner's decision. Other STEPs remove their own personal data through the registered anonymize handlers.
- D9 (08.3) Bulk actions (archive, reactivate, delete, add or remove a tag, set risk, set payment terms) take up to 200 clients, show the count before applying, handle each client separately and report how many were done and why the others were not. Each kind of change also needs its own permission. The statement e-mail template is `invoice.client-statement` (the `invoice.` prefix lets a company edit the wording and keeps the unsubscribe header off documents).
